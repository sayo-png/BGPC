Mission Statement

BestGuard Termite & Pest Control was created with the objective aims of providing targeted pest extermination service to residentials and commercial businesses protection against common pests,rodents and any wood-destroying insects like termites, carpenter bees and carpenter ants. BestGuard is centered on delivering dependable protection that secure our customers homes and businesses through integrated pest management.

Some of the services we provide include:

  • Detailed interior and exterior treatments for ants, spiders, roaches, crickets, sting bugs, ticks and fleas.
  • Thorough termites inspection and treatments, which may consist of liquid barrier treatment or baits installation.
  • Rodents exclusion services.

We are focused on providing reliable, eco-friendly, and comprehensive pest management to protect homes and businesses. Our focus is our customers needs.
